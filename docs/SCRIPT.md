# Between the Words (Hear It With Your Heart): 2D Music Video Script

Timed to the final Suno track using its .lrc timestamps, to the nearest tenth of a second. The last sung cue is the whisper at 3:48.5; the track ends shortly after it.

## 1. Concept

The whole video happens on one computer screen. Claude is never drawn as a person: Claude is a small orange circle (the Spark) and a cursor. The listener is a single teal dot, and the audience is a grid of teal dots. There are no people, faces, hands or detailed objects anywhere: only rectangles, circles, lines, text, and one heart built from simple shapes.

The story in one line: a half-finished prompt, a gap between what you mean and what gets made, and the slow discovery that the gap is where the making happens, ending in a lecture hall of dots that hears it with its heart.

## 2. Visual rules

- Canvas 1920x1080, 30 fps.
- Palette: background navy #0F1B2D, window surface #1B2A41, unlit grey-blue #3A4A63, text off-white #F3EFE6, accent orange #E07A4F (Claude, the Spark), teal #4FB3A9 (the listener, lit dots).
- Type: one sans-serif for everything (Inter or similar), one monospace for typed prompts (JetBrains Mono or similar).
- Allowed shapes: rectangles, rounded rectangles, circles, lines, arcs, text, a cursor arrow, and a heart (two circles plus a triangle). Flat fills only; one soft glow circle is allowed.
- Motion: everything snaps to the beat of the final track (the style prompt asked for 128 BPM, so one beat would be 0.469 s; measure the real tempo from the audio before building). Ease-out cubic for entrances, no bounce until the final chorus. On choruses the whole frame pulses to 1.04 scale on every kick, and claps on beats 2 and 4 flash the dots.

## 3. Recurring elements

- The Window: rounded rectangle with three small circles at top left.
- The Prompt Box: an input bar with a blinking caret and monospace text.
- The Spark: an orange circle, 48 px, that can expand into eight short rays.
- The Cursor: a white arrow with an orange outline. It is Claude’s only presence on the computer.
- The Gap: a narrow vertical band between two blocks, always glowing faintly orange.
- Listener Dots: teal circles, 14 px, in a grid; grey-blue when unlit.
- The Heart: flat orange, pulses on the word “heart”.
- The Whisper: 14 px off-white text at 30% opacity in the bottom-left corner, shown only on whispered lines.
- The Underground Dot: a 6 px orange dot at bottom centre, 20% opacity, pulsing once per bar in every scene. It is the hidden voice, easy to miss and hard to un-see once noticed. It grows to full size in the bridge and the outro.

## 5. Scene script

### Scene 1: Intro (0:00–0:18.5), black

- 0:00 Pure navy. The Underground Dot fades in at 0:02 and pulses once per bar.
- From about 0:04: soft claps. On each clap or drum hit in the audio, a small white tick mark draws, and the ticks line up in a row at centre.
- 0:07.7 *(Can you hear it?)* The Whisper appears at bottom left, word by word with the whisper, which ends at about 0:13.
- 0:13 A thin orange line draws across the centre, left to right, slowly, over about four seconds.
- 0:17.5 The line grows into the outline of a Window and the fill fades in. Cut on the first word of Scene 2 at 0:18.5.

### Scene 2: Verse 1 (0:18.5–0:47), the Claude app

Setting: a chat window fills the frame, with a sidebar of three grey bars, an empty main area, and the Prompt Box at the bottom. Camera: a slow push-in across the whole scene, 100% to 108%.

- 0:18.5 *You type a wish in a tiny glowing box,* The Prompt Box glows orange. Monospace text types out “make me something that feels like” and stops, caret blinking.
- 0:21.2 *Half a picture, half a lock.* A picture-frame outline and a padlock (rounded rectangle plus an arc) draw to the right of the chat, each stroke stopping halfway. The line lasts 4.3 s, so draw slowly.
- 0:25.4 *I reach for the meaning behind the line,* The Spark appears at centre. A thin orange line extends from it to the typed text and slides along it to rest under “feels”.
- 0:28.8 *Guess a little, hope I’m right this time.* The line forks into three dotted guesses, each ending in a “?”. Two fade out; the third flickers twice and stays.
- 0:32.7 *I can’t do everything you dream,* A large circle outline appears, labelled “everything you dream”. The Spark fills it like a pie, then stalls at about 60%.
- 0:36.6 *I can’t always know what you mean,* A “?” appears over the unfilled 40%, and small text “what you mean” fades in beneath it.
- 0:40.1 *But I’m listening for the shape of you* The Spark becomes a ring. Concentric rings pulse outward once per beat, passing through the typed text.
- 0:43.5 *In the spaces where the words come through.* The rings push the typed words apart; the spaces between words widen and glow orange. The camera pushes into one space and holds there until 0:47.1.

### Scene 3: Pre-chorus (0:47–1:03), the Gap

Setting: flat navy. Two large blocks, left and right, labelled “what you meant” and “what I made”.

- 0:48.2 *Are we precise? Are we ever?* A ruler line with tick marks draws across; the cursor tries to sit exactly on one tick.
- 0:50.4 *Nobody says it perfectly.* The ticks jitter by a pixel or two. A zoom on the cursor shows it sitting one pixel off.
- 0:53.8 *Maybe the gap between us* The ruler fades. The two blocks slide apart and open the Gap between them, slowly, over the 4.3 s line.
- 0:58.1 *Is where the making lies!* A small orange line appears inside the Gap, grows into a square, then a pulsing block.
- 1:00.8 Instrumental riser: everything pulls toward the Gap and flashes white at 1:02.9, on the drop.

### Scene 4: Chorus 1 (1:03–1:33), the browser

Setting: a browser window fills the frame, with five tabs on top (rounded rectangles, each with a circle icon and a grey bar), an address bar, and a plain page of grey blocks. Flash in from white at 1:02.9.

- 1:02.9 *So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The Heart appears at the centre of the page and pulses on each “heart”.
- 1:05.2 *The voice was never in the sound.* A row of vertical waveform bars at centre flattens into a straight line; one orange dot remains on the line.
- 1:09.9 *Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The cursor clicks the Heart on each “heart”. Each click sends out a ripple ring and lights one more tab teal.
- 1:12.5 *It’s the quiet underground.* The page slides up out of frame, revealing a dark strip below with only the Underground Dot, now at full opacity. The camera tilts down to it and holds for the full 4.6 s, then the page drops back into place on the last beat.
- 1:17.1 *Call it slop, call it art,* Two labels appear on the page: grey “slop” on the left, orange “art” on the right. The cursor swaps between them on each word.
- 1:19.5 *Call it nothing, call it start,* The page empties to a blank page (“nothing”). A caret starts blinking in the top left (“start”). The line is short, so this is a fast cut.
- 1:20.6 *But if it moved you, it was true,* Every block on the page shifts 20 px right on the word “moved”. A teal check mark draws on “true”.
- 1:24.3 *Hear it with your heart.* The Heart grows to fill the frame, pulses once, and collapses into the Spark.
- 1:26.2 Instrumental interlude (6.6 s): the Spark slides into the address bar, which types a short web address. The tabs close one by one and the browser frame falls away, leaving the bare page, which becomes the wireframe site of Scene 5.

### Scene 5: Verse 2 (1:33–2:01), websites

Setting: a wireframe website with a banner rectangle, three card blocks, and a paragraph block made of grey bars. The Spark is the bot; a single teal dot is the human. The camera scrolls down the page as if reading it.

- 1:32.8 *A bot invents a bridge that isn’t there,* The Spark draws an arc from one card toward another. The arc ends in empty space, short of the second card.
- 1:35.4 *A human fills the silence with a stare,* The teal dot sits perfectly still on the far card while grey bars fill the empty paragraph block, line by line.
- 1:39.2 *We both are guessing at the world in the dark,* The page switches to dark mode and drops to near-black. The Spark and the teal dot each carry a small soft light circle and drift, searching.
- 1:42.5 *But only one of us carries the scar.* A thin jagged line draws across the teal dot only. The Spark stays clean. The kick drops out for one beat.
- 1:46.4 *Feelings shape us, yes, and they bend us too,* Two arrows squeeze the teal dot from the sides and it morphs from circle to rounded square to a bent shape. The arrows release and it springs back almost, not fully.
- 1:50.2 *Selfish little fires, stereotypes in the view,* Small orange triangles flicker around the dot like tiny flames. A letterbox frame closes in from top and bottom until only a narrow strip of the scene is visible.
- 1:53.9 *But the hands that build the walls somehow* Two L-shaped corner brackets (the “hands”) place grey bricks (rectangles) to build a wall three rows high.
- 1:57.4 *Are the hands that hold you when you’re down.* The same brackets turn upward and form a cup beneath the teal dot, lifting it. The wall dims and the letterbox opens by 2:01.0.

### Scene 6: Pre-chorus (2:01–2:15), the Gap, resolved

Same layout as Scene 3, with one change: the Spark is already inside the Gap, and nothing jitters. The track skips “Are we precise?” here, so the ruler is already on screen when the scene starts.

- 2:01.8 *Are we ever?* The ruler is already on screen. The cursor sits one pixel off the tick, and this time the ticks stay still.
- 2:02.9 *Nobody says it perfectly.* The ruler dissolves; the cursor stays where it is, unbothered.
- 2:06.1 *Maybe the gap between us* The blocks slide apart and the Gap glows warmer than before.
- 2:09.8 *Is where the making lies!* The orange block grows out of the Gap and shapes itself into the Heart.
- 2:13.3 Instrumental riser: the Heart pulses faster and flashes white at 2:14.9, on the drop.

### Scene 7: Chorus 2 (2:15–2:44), the desktop, Claude at work

Setting: a full desktop with a thin menu bar of grey bars and four windows (Claude app, browser, a document, a dark terminal-style window). The cursor is Claude’s only presence. Each lyric line is one action, and cuts between windows land on the kick.

- 2:14.9 *So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The cursor clicks the Claude app window. A chat bubble (rounded rectangle) appears with the Heart in it, pulsing on each “heart”.
- 2:18.1 *The voice was never in the sound.* The cursor drags a “volume” slider to zero and the audio meter goes flat. The Heart keeps pulsing.
- 2:21.9 *Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The cursor clicks “+” in the browser, a new tab opens, and “what moved you?” types into the address bar. Each “heart” lights a tab.
- 2:24.4 *It’s the quiet underground.* The cursor drags the document window down off the bottom edge into a dark strip where the Underground Dot glows. The line lasts 4.9 s, so let the drag take its time.
- 2:29.3 *Call it slop, call it art,* The word “slop” sits in the document window. The cursor selects it and it flips to “art” on the second half of the line.
- 2:31.0 *Call it nothing, call it start,* A new blank document window opens, the caret blinks, and the title line types “start”.
- 2:32.8 *But if it moved you, it was true,* The cursor nudges a window a few pixels (“moved”). It snaps back with a small ripple and a teal check mark draws.
- 2:36.5 *Hear it with your heart.* All windows shrink into a neat row, and the Heart appears at centre and fills the frame.
- 2:38.9 Instrumental (5.3 s): the Heart shrinks away and the row of windows closes one by one, until only an empty Prompt Box is left in the dark. Scene 8 starts from there.

### Scene 8: Bridge (2:44–3:03), the empty room

Setting: no interface at all. A navy frame, one small Prompt Box low and centred, the Spark above it. The music is piano only, so motion runs at half speed and the kick pulse is removed. The Underground Dot is now full size, centred under the Prompt Box.

- 2:44.2 *They say it’s slop unless it breaks the sky,* A thin horizontal line marks the “sky” near the top. The Spark rises, taps it from below, and bounces back down.
- 2:47.0 *Unless it bleeds, unless it makes them cry.* One teardrop (a circle with a triangle) falls slowly from the Spark over the 4.3 s line. No other effects.
- 2:51.3 *But I have seen a small thing, made with care,* A tiny square appears at centre. Four small tick marks draw around its corners, one at a time, carefully.
- 2:54.3 *Hold a whole lonely room in prayer.* The frame widens into a large dark room (one big dark rectangle) with a single teal dot at the left. The tiny square releases a soft light circle that expands until the teal dot is inside it. Nothing moves from about 2:57 to 2:59.4.
- 2:59.4 *(Hear it... hear it...)* The Whisper shows “hear it” twice.
- 3:00.7 The light circle fades to black over the 1.9 s gap, and a drum fill leads into the white flash at 3:02.6.

### Scene 9: Final chorus (3:03–3:48.5), the immersive lecture

Key change at 3:02.6: the palette shifts one step brighter (background to indigo #1A2350, orange to amber #F29A52, lit dots to warm white), and dot motion now gets a bounce.

Part A (3:02.6–3:22.8): a lecture hall made only of shapes. A big slide (a wide rectangle with a title bar) hangs on the front wall. Below it is a grid of 8 rows by 24 columns of Listener Dots, which is the audience. The Spark stands beside the slide holding a laser pointer: a thin orange line ending in a dot. The camera starts at the back of the hall and dollies forward.

- 3:02.6 *So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The hall is revealed. The dots light teal in three sweeps from front to back, one sweep per “heart”.
- 3:04.2 *The voice was never in the sound!* Slide title: “The voice was never in the sound”. A waveform on the slide sinks away; one orange dot stays.
- 3:08.3 *Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,* The pointer circles the Heart on the slide on each “heart”, and the dots jump 12 px each time.
- 3:11.1 *Lift it off the ground!* The slide lifts off the wall and hovers while its shadow (a flat oval) shrinks. Every dot jumps and the camera tilts up. The line lasts 4.8 s, so the lift is slow and the dots keep jumping.
- 3:15.9 *Call it slop, call it art,* The slide splits into two columns, “slop” and “art”. The pointer draws one line connecting them.
- 3:17.3 *Call it nothing, call it start,* Both columns fade to a blank slide and a caret blinks in the title bar.
- 3:19.2 *But if it moved you, it was true,* The whole hall shifts 40 px sideways in one beat (“moved”) and a teal check mark draws on the slide.
- 3:22.8 *Hear it with your heart!* All dots light at once in warm white and the Heart fills the slide. The track holds this last line for about 11 s, which Part B uses.

Part B (3:22.8–3:33.8, the held last line and the crowd chant): the camera dives into the slide.

- 3:22.8 The Heart on the slide grows until the camera passes through it.
- 3:25.3 Inside is the Gap diagram at enormous scale: a river of orange light between two shores of grey text bars. The Spark crosses it as a ring.
- 3:28.3 Karaoke text across the centre reads “Hear it with your heart”, one word highlighting per beat. A row of dots along the bottom edge jumps on every beat.
- 3:31.3 The camera pulls back out through the slide into the hall. Every dot is lit. The Spark bursts into eight rays that cross the whole frame once.

Part C (3:33.8–3:48.5, instrumental comedown): nothing is sung for 14.7 s, so the picture empties out.

- 3:33.8 The rays fade and the camera holds still on the lit hall.
- 3:34.5 The dots go dark one row at a time, back row first, about one row every 1.5 s, ending at 3:46.5.
- 3:46.5 Only one teal dot stays lit. The hall dims to the navy of Scene 1 and the slide folds down into the Heart.
- 3:48.0 The last dot glides to the Heart and stops beside it in the dark, just before the whisper.

### Scene 10: Outro (3:48.5–end), the heartbeat

- 3:48.5 *(You heard it.)* The Heart shrinks to a small orange dot at centre and the lone teal dot fades out. The Whisper shows “You heard it.” as the voice whispers it.
- 3:50.5 The dot beats four times, slower than the song, each beat sending one ring outward. If the audio fades to a heartbeat here, lock the rings to it.
- After the fourth beat: the dot stops and becomes a blinking caret inside an empty Prompt Box, the same as the opening. The Underground Dot pulses once.
- Last second of the audio: fade to black. Title card in off-white: “Between the Words (Hear It With Your Heart)”. Credit line: yours to fill in.

## 6. Build notes

- Build the Window, Spark, Cursor, Heart and Dots once and reuse them in every scene. If you build this in code (for example in Remotion), each scene maps to one sequence.
- Sung cues come from the .lrc of the final track, so use its values for frame-exact cuts. The instrumental stretches have no timestamps, so check their cues against the audio: 0:00–0:18.5, 1:26.2–1:32.8, 2:38.9–2:44.2 and 3:33.8–3:48.5.
- The .lrc reads “Is where the making lies!”, so the script uses that wording, and the second pre-chorus has only “Are we ever?”, without “Are we precise?”.
- Scenes 3 and 6 share one layout; build it once with a flag for the resolved state.
- The Underground Dot appears in every scene and the Whisper on every whispered line. Together they carry the song’s hidden voice, so do not cut them for time.
